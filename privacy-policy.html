Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

Sleep Well Sounds is developed and provided by Muhammad Ilyas | RapidGlobal.

This Privacy Policy explains how Sleep Well Sounds ("the App") handles information when you use the application.

By using Sleep Well Sounds, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Sleep Well Sounds is designed to provide its core functionality without requiring you to create an account or provide personal information.

The current version of the App does not intentionally collect personal information such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home or physical address
  • Contacts
  • Photos or personal files
  • Precise location
  • Passwords or account credentials
  • Payment information
  • Microphone recordings
  • Voice recordings

The core functionality of the App operates locally on your device.

2. Audio and Microphone

Sleep Well Sounds provides pre-installed ambient sounds such as rainfall, breeze, and thunder.

The App does not use your device microphone to record, listen to, monitor, or upload your voice or surrounding sounds.

The ambient audio played by the App consists of audio files provided as part of the application.

3. How Sleep Well Sounds Works

The App allows you to:

  • Play and pause ambient sounds
  • Mix multiple sounds
  • Adjust individual sound volumes
  • Use a sleep timer
  • Continue audio playback in the background
  • Use the core sound experience without an internet connection

These core functions are processed on your device.

4. Local Storage and Preferences

The App may store certain settings or preferences locally on your device to provide a better user experience.

Examples may include application preferences or sound-related settings.

Information stored locally by the App is not intentionally transmitted to our servers.

5. Internet Connection

The core sound experience is designed to work without requiring an internet connection.

The App may require an internet connection for certain future features, updates, or services if such functionality is introduced.

If a future feature changes how information is collected, used, or shared, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or intentionally share your personal information with third parties.

The current version of Sleep Well Sounds does not intentionally use third-party services for the purpose of collecting personal information from users.

7. Analytics and Tracking

The current version of Sleep Well Sounds does not intentionally use analytics or tracking technologies to build personal profiles of users.

If analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or other third-party services are introduced in a future version and those services collect or process user information, the relevant privacy disclosures will be updated.

8. Advertising

The privacy practices relating to advertising depend on whether advertising services are included in the version of the App you are using.

If advertising or an advertising SDK is introduced, the applicable data collection and sharing practices will be disclosed in an updated version of this Privacy Policy and in the relevant Google Play disclosures.

9. Children's Privacy

Sleep Well Sounds does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

The App does not require users to create an account or submit personal information to use its core functionality.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us using the information provided below.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information associated with the App.

Because the current core functionality does not require us to maintain a personal user database, Sleep Well Sounds does not maintain a server-side database of users' personal information for its core functionality.

However, no electronic system or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Third-Party Services

The current version of Sleep Well Sounds does not intentionally use third-party services to collect personal information for the core functionality of the App.

The App may depend on software libraries or platform services necessary for normal application operation.

If third-party services are introduced that collect or process user information, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the relevant practices.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Sleep Well Sounds does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information for its core functionality, we do not maintain a personal user account database requiring deletion.

Information stored locally on your device can generally be removed by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App, depending on your device and operating system.

If a future version introduces accounts or server-side personal information, appropriate data retention and deletion procedures will be described in an updated Privacy Policy.

13. Your Privacy Choices

You can control your use of the App by:

  • Choosing whether to use the App
  • Adjusting available app settings
  • Clearing the App's local data through your device settings
  • Uninstalling the App

The App does not currently require you to create an account or submit personal information to use its core functionality.

14. Links to Other Websites

The App or this website may contain links to external websites or services.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of external websites.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external service before providing personal information.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, new features, third-party services, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

When changes are made, the Last Updated date at the top of this Privacy Policy will be updated.

We encourage users to review this page periodically.

16. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Sleep Well Sounds, please contact us.
